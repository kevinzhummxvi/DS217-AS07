Line chart showing follow-up visit number (visits 1 through 4) on the x-axis and the percentage of patients meeting the weekly exercise goal on the y-axis.For the Home-based program, from visit 1 to visit 4, goal attainment increases from 58% to 70%. For the Center-based program, it increases from 57% to 79%.At visit 4, the Center-based program is 9 percentage points higher than the Home-based program, 79% versus 70%This difference is descriptive, patients were not randomized for they chose their program, so the gap does not show that one program causes more exercise.Each percentage covers only the patients seen at that visit, about 45 per program, so a 9-point gap could also come from chance or from who returned.
